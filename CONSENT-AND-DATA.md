# Consent and data handling

## Survey A: students and graduates (anonymous, n = 57)
Collected via Microsoft Forms, 22 to 24 September 2026, open link circulated within NIBM. No names or
emails were collected in the exported analysis set. Only aggregates and cross-tabulations are stored here.
The Sri Lankan Personal Data Protection Act applies to the source workbook, which stays in NIBM's
internal storage, not in this repository.

## Survey B: industry and external academics (named, n = 7)
Respondents gave names and organisations for the stated purpose of informing programme design. The
deployed form did not include a quotation-consent item. Therefore:

- Quotations in the deck are attributed by sector only ("an IT services practitioner").
- Names and organisations are not published here; `survey-b-industry-academics-results.md` is the
  internal analysis and should be replaced by an anonymised version before any external release.
- Attributed quotation requires a one-line email confirmation from the respondent, filed in
  `verification/`.

## Named academics on the delivery slide
Titles and research descriptions are taken from public university pages and Google Scholar or DBLP
(see `evidence/nibm/delivery-panel.md`). Consent to be named in a proposal to Essex is tracked in
`verification/log.md`.

## Institutional records
Convocation counts and year-3 intake counts are NIBM records supplied by SOCE administration and are
reproduced as aggregates only.

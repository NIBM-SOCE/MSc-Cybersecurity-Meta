# MSc Cyber Security at NIBM: evidence pack for the University of Essex

This repository holds the business case for franchising the University of Essex MSc Cyber Security,
with a Postgraduate Diploma exit award, to the National Institute of Business Management (NIBM),
School of Computing and Engineering, Colombo. It is organised so that every figure on every slide can
be traced to a source in a few clicks.

Prepared by Niranga Dharmaratna, academic delivery and programme coordination, NIBM SOCE.
Contractual and partnership contact with Essex is handled by a separate NIBM team.

## What is here

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| `deck/` | The presentation (14 slides) and a separate appendix deck (7 slides, held for questions), both as PPTX and PDF, plus the generator script that rebuilds both (`DECK=main` or `DECK=appendix`) |
| `docs/` | Speaker script with timings and a question bank; the deck architecture and reasoning; a figure audit tracing every number on every slide to its source |
| `evidence/surveys/` | Aggregated results of the two feasibility surveys (students and graduates, n = 57; industry and external academics, n = 7) and the synthesis used in the deck |
| `evidence/nibm/` | Institutional facts, convocation and intake records, delivery panel profiles with public sources |
| `evidence/essex/` | The Essex facts relied on: partnership principles, programme profile, fees, award structure, rankings |
| `evidence/market/` | Competitor matrix (Sri Lankan cyber master's provision), league-table positions, fee anchors, exchange rate and labour-market sources |
| `verification/` | The open and closed verification items, with owners |

## How to read it

Start with `docs/deck-architecture.md` for the argument, then the deck. Every slide's speaker note names
the evidence file that backs it. `verification/log.md` lists what is still open and who owns it.

## What is deliberately not here

Row-level survey data and respondent names. Survey A is anonymous; Survey B respondents consented to
inform programme design, not to attributed publication. See `CONSENT-AND-DATA.md`. Nothing in this
repository is a fee proposal for the NIBM programme; that depends on Essex's commercial terms.

## Sharing

Share a tagged release, not the working branch. The release contains no drafts, no internal
discussion, and no personal data beyond the named academics' public professional profiles.

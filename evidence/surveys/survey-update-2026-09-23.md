The seventh response, from a life insurer, expects demand to rise significantly, rates a UK master's a
clear advantage, and asks for offensive and defensive practice, governance, cloud, security operations
and AI automation as course content. The one "no difference" answer reflects promotion within the
state-university system and is reported in the counts without further emphasis.

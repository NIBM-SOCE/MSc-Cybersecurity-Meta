## Exchange rate
GBP/LKR about 445 to 452 in July and August 2026 (OFX, poundsterlinglive). 2022 range 264 to 461; 2026
year-to-date average about 431. Source: ofx.com and currencyconvert.online historical tables.

## Labour market
- Entry-level IT salaries from about LKR 80,000 a month; senior and specialised roles above LKR 600,000
  (ITPro.lk salary guide, June 2026).
- SLASSCOM: about 17,000 IT graduates a year (Nov 2025); entry-level supply no longer the bottleneck
  (Ceylon Today, "Sri Lanka's AI Jobpocalypse?", 12 Apr 2026).
- IFS laid off 400 to 450 staff in Sri Lanka in waves from November 2025, citing AI implementations; QA
  and technical-writing roles most exposed; global tech layoffs above 245,000 in 2025 (The Examiner,
  "Sri Lankan techies are cheap, AI is cheaper", 15 May 2026).

## Bridge credentials
- ISC2 concluded its One Million Certified in Cybersecurity programme (free CC course and exam) on
  20 May 2026; the CC exam is now a paid voucher, listed at about USD 299 (isc2.org, 22 Apr 2026;
  itmasters.edu.au listing).
- CompTIA Security+ and EC-Council Cybersecurity Essentials: paid; prices to be confirmed at the time of
  pricing lane two.

## British-system familiarity
- National examinations are GCE O/L and A/L. The British Council Sri Lanka administers Cambridge and
  Pearson Edexcel school examinations for schools and private candidates (britishcouncil.lk).
- Lyceum International School network alone: 25,000+ students, Edexcel for its international stream
  (Wikipedia, 2026). No national count of Edexcel candidates was found; do not state one.
